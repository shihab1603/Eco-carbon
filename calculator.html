Understand Your Impact

What Contributes to Your Carbon Footprint?

Different daily activities can contribute to greenhouse gas emissions. Understanding the major categories can help identify areas for improvement.

Energy

Electricity and fuel used in homes, offices and facilities can contribute to overall emissions.

Transport

Cars, motorcycles, public transport and other mobility activities can affect emissions.

Travel

Air travel can represent a significant part of an individual's or organization's footprint.

Waste

Waste generation and disposal practices can contribute to greenhouse gas emissions.

A footprint estimate is only the first step. Better data can help organizations and individuals identify practical opportunities for reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

  • Consider lower-emission transportation
  • Reduce unnecessary resource consumption
  • Improve waste management
