Carbon Credit Buyer

Who is a Carbon Credit Buyer?

A carbon credit buyer is an organization, company, institution or other eligible participant that purchases carbon credits from carbon projects to support climate action, meet sustainability objectives or manage carbon-related commitments. Eco-Carbon helps buyers discover projects, compare opportunities, communicate with sellers and move toward informed carbon credit procurement.
