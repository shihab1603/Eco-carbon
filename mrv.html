MRV & Verification

Making Carbon Project Data More Reliable

Measurement, Reporting and Verification (MRV) is an important part of understanding and documenting the environmental performance of a carbon project.

Eco-Carbon supports project stakeholders with structured approaches to data collection, monitoring, reporting, documentation and verification readiness.

  • Project monitoring and data collection
  • Emission reduction and carbon data management
  • Reporting and documentation support
  • Verification readiness and coordination
Our MRV Framework

Four Key Areas of MRV

A structured MRV approach helps project teams manage information consistently throughout the project lifecycle.

Measurement

Identify relevant project data and establish appropriate approaches for measuring project activities and outcomes.

Monitoring

Track project activities, operational information, field observations and other relevant indicators.

Reporting

Organize collected information into structured records and reports for project stakeholders.

Verification

Support the preparation and coordination of documentation required for applicable verification processes.

Project Data

From Field Data to Project Records

Good project data should be organized, traceable and accessible to the people responsible for monitoring and reporting.

01
Data Collection

Collect relevant project information from field activities, equipment, sensors, surveys and other appropriate sources.

02
Data Management

Organize project records, supporting documents and monitoring information in a structured environment.

03
Data Review

Review information for consistency, completeness and documentation readiness before reporting or verification activities.

MRV Process

How MRV Support Works

A practical workflow can help project teams prepare, manage and document their MRV activities.

01

Project Assessment

Understand the project, activities, available data and applicable requirements.

02

MRV Planning

Define monitoring activities, data requirements, responsibilities and reporting structure.

03

Data Collection

Gather relevant project data through appropriate field and digital monitoring methods.

04

Reporting

Prepare organized records and reports based on available project information.

05

Verification Support

Prepare documentation and coordinate information required for applicable verification activities.

Verification Readiness

Verification can require clear evidence, consistent records and supporting documentation. Eco-Carbon can help project teams organize information before an applicable verification process.

  • Project documentation organization
  • Monitoring records
  • Supporting evidence and data
  • Reporting structure
  • Verification coordination support

What We Help Organize

The exact requirements depend on the project, methodology, applicable standard and verification arrangement. Our approach focuses on organized, traceable project information.

Project Records

Organize project documents and relevant supporting records.

Monitoring Evidence

Maintain appropriate monitoring and measurement information.

Reporting Documents

Structure reporting materials and relevant project evidence.

Verification Coordination

Help coordinate information exchange during the applicable verification process.
