Responsible Project Development

A carbon project requires more than an idea. Clear project boundaries, reliable data, appropriate methodologies, monitoring systems, documentation and verification processes can all be important parts of project development.

Eco-Carbon aims to help project owners understand these requirements and organize the information needed for the next stage of their project.

Project eligibility, crediting potential, certification and market outcomes depend on the applicable methodology, standard, project design and verification requirements.
