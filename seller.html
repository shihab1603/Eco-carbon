Project Opportunities

What Can You Bring to Eco-Carbon?

Carbon projects can come from different sectors and technologies. Each project can be assessed based on its characteristics, methodology, documentation and verification requirements.

Nature-Based Projects

Forestry, reforestation, restoration, conservation and other nature-focused climate projects.

Renewable Energy

Renewable energy and clean-energy initiatives that contribute to emission reduction.

Emission Reduction

Projects designed to reduce greenhouse gas emissions through improved processes and technologies.

Waste Management

Waste reduction, recycling, methane avoidance and resource-efficiency related initiatives.

Clean Water & Community

Projects combining climate action with environmental and community development benefits.

Technology & Innovation

Technology-driven solutions that support measurable emission reduction or carbon removal activities.

Get Ready

Information You May Need

Having clear project information helps make the assessment and market onboarding process more efficient.

Project Information

  • Project name and location
  • Project type and sector
  • Project description and objectives
  • Project implementation details
  • Expected emission reduction or removal information

Credit Information

  • Applicable methodology or standard
  • Verification and issuance information
  • Credit vintage and available volume
  • Project monitoring information
  • Supporting records and documentation
